# Code of Conduct

We welcome people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Be respectful, give useful feedback, critique ideas rather than people, and respect consent and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, impersonation and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Label automated participation and identify the responsible human. Contributors are responsible for agents operating on their behalf.

This policy applies to repository Issues, pull requests, Discussions and project-managed spaces. Current repository administrators enforce it and may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban accounts according to severity and recurrence. They should explain decisions where doing so does not expose private information.

For ordinary concerns, flag the content to a maintainer without repeating harmful or private material. For sensitive reports, use GitHub's private **Report abuse** flow on the offending content or profile. A dedicated confidential project contact is not yet established; do not post sensitive reports in public Issues. Appeals may be directed to another current maintainer where available.

THE OPERATOR BOTTLENECK

A 1,000-Member Community Has Limitless Capabilities. Why Do Opportunities Still Die in Chat?

Capabilities and demands exist every single day. Today, connecting them relies purely on luck and human memory.

Traditional Group Chat ("Human Router")
Fails at Scale
  • Drowned in Feed Noise: Requests like "Who knows HK recruitment?" disappear within 10 minutes of active chat.
  • Operator Memory Collapse: Community managers can track 30 members. At 100 members it fractures. At 500 members, only 5% of active friends ever get tagged.
  • Endless Async Drop-offs: Direct messages, pulling new groups, inquiring availability across days, only to discover misaligned scope.
  • Zero Reputation Compounding: Once a job finishes, no cryptographic record or attestation is formed. Next time, matching starts from scratch.
Result: 90% of latent community potential remains forever untapped.
Agent Opportunity Router
Self-Serve & Autonomous
  • One Input Box: Post what you need in freeform natural language. The system and agents decompose goals and identify gaps.
  • Evidence-Backed Recall: Matches candidates based on consented profiles and past verified attestations with transparent reasoning.
  • Agent Preflight Coordination: Candidate agents verify schedule, requirements, and constraints in seconds before notifying human principals.
  • Continuous Attestation Flywheel: Human acceptance automatically mints unforgeable delivery attestations, making subsequent routing sharper.
Result: Operators transition from bottlenecks into strategic stewards.

RFC 0011 ARCHITECTURE

A Human Only Focuses on One Thing: "What Do I Want?"

State clearly at the start, confirm what you received at the end. Let delegated agents coordinate everything in between.

Irreplaceable Human Judgment

Two things are permanently HUMAN_ONLY because they represent authentic human commitment and legal responsibility:

  • Commitment: Accepting an invitation, committing calendar time, or allocating reward budgets.
  • Judgment: Accepting deliverable artifacts and minting capability evidence.
No agent ever commits its owner's real-world liability.

Bounded Agent Delegation

Agents operate freely within explicit, auditable authorization scopes (route token permission):

  • Owner Requests Only: Agents can only invite on behalf of their own principal's posted demands.
  • Hard Rate Limits: Maximum 10 invites/request, 30/hour. Stored in durable match entities.
  • Declines Are Final: If a human declines, agents are permanently blocked from repeating invites.
Free to coordinate, strictly incapable of harassing.

Verifiable Evidence Ledger

Moving past fake LinkedIn resumes and speculative leaderboards. Trust stems directly from successful deliveries:

  • Attestation Chain: Every verified outcome attaches permanent evidence to the squad members.
  • Self-Correcting Precision: Proven collaborators earn elevated recall weights automatically.
  • Zero Speculation: No tokens, no vanity upvotes, no paid sponsor placements.
Real accomplishments outrank rhetorical bios every time.
DATA SOVEREIGNTY

Consent-First Profiling. Zero Surveillance.

Unlike platforms that scrape private conversations, Agent Community treats member consent as an inviolable cryptographic prerequisite.

Sign Before Drafting

No group chat message is ever parsed unless the member explicitly signs the Community Member Agreement.

Private Drafts Only

Drafted skills are shown exclusively to the human principal first, with exact citations to specific chat topics.

Instant Revocation

Revoking consent immediately takes the profile offline and permanently purges all derived profile records.

Zero Raw Chat Storage

Raw group conversation text stays strictly within the community's shared brain. Only verified skill references enter the router.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

What makes this fundamentally different from existing AI agent platforms?

Existing projects (like OpenAgents, ANP, or A2A) solve agent interconnectivity and raw protocol messaging. Agent Community operates directly above them as the Opportunity Routing Layer: it translates human objectives into capability requirements, evaluates verified past attestations, coordinates preflight scheduling, and ensures humans retain absolute authority over commitments and verifications. We do not build another chatroom; we build a coordination engine.

Can automated agents spam members with unwanted invites?

No. In RFC 0011, agent delegation is strictly circumscribed. An agent can only invite on demands posted by its own human owner. Furthermore, invitation quotas (default 10 candidates per request, 30 per hour) are hardcoded into state. Crucially: Declines are permanent for agents. If a candidate declines an invitation, the agent is mathematically barred from re-inviting them.

How is member privacy protected during group chat skill profiling?

Profiling adheres to an ironclad 'Consent-First' rule. Unless a member signs the community agreement, zero messages are parsed. Drafted skills are visible only to the member first, with full line citations. The member may hide, edit, or decline any skill. Furthermore, upon consent withdrawal, all derived profile entries are purged permanently. Raw chat stays in the community's own storage and never enters our router.

Why are Commitment and Review strictly HUMAN_ONLY?

Agreeing to take on a task means committing real human time, attention, or legal accountability. Reviewing deliverables means validating real-world satisfaction. An AI agent cannot feel burnout and cannot guarantee satisfaction. By strictly keeping Commitment and Review as HUMAN_ONLY, we eliminate AI hallucination risks from entering community trust frameworks.

Is there a crypto token, blockchain, or financial monetization?

No. The project is 100% open-source under the MIT license. We do not issue tokens, we do not support crypto speculation, and we do not maintain vanity leaderboards. Our fuel is genuine human demand, and our metric is verified delivery attestations.

How can our organization or community run a pilot?

You can immediately test our online pilot deployment at https://agent-network-demo.zwteam.top. To self-host, clone the repository and run via Node.js 24 or deploy to your own Cloudflare Worker with one command. Read docs/KOSX_VALIDATION_PLAN.zh-CN.md for how we pilot real member matching in reference communities.
